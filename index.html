Selected publications

My research explores how motion priors can be adapted to changing commands, terrain, and physical conditions through parameter optimization, residual policies, and motion generation. Research projects ↗ Paper explainers ↗

RA-L 2026

Learning Whole-Body Humanoid Locomotion via Motion Generation and Motion Tracking

Zewei Zhang, Kehan Wen, Michael Xu, Junzhe He, Chenhao Li, Takahiro Miki, Clemens Schwarke, Chong Zhang, Xue Bin Peng, Marco Hutter

A diffusion model generates terrain-aware references from human motion, while an RL-trained tracker executes them on a Unitree G1 with onboard perception and computation.

CoRL 2025

Motion Priors Reimagined: Adapting Flat-Terrain Skills for Complex Quadruped Mobility

Zewei Zhang, Chenhao Li, Takahiro Miki, Marco Hutter

A high-level policy combines learned dog locomotion skills with joint-level residual corrections for terrain traversal and local navigation on ANYmal-D.

▶ Explainer · 1:50 Paper Project

IROS 2024 · Oral presentation

Online Optimization of Central Pattern Generators for Quadruped Locomotion

Zewei Zhang, Guillaume Bellegarda, Milad Shafiee, Auke Ijspeert

Online Bayesian optimization adapts a CPG-based controller to changes in velocity commands, ground friction, slope, and payload on a Unitree Go1.
